# Health & Safety Disclaimer

FormFit provides general fitness information and coaching. It is **not** a medical device and does
not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

- Talk to a doctor before starting any exercise programme, especially if you have heart disease,
  high blood pressure, diabetes, joint, bone or back problems, a recent injury or surgery, are
  pregnant or have recently given birth, are over 65 and inactive, or answered "yes" to any
  PAR-Q+ question.
- **Stop immediately** and seek medical help if you feel chest pain or pressure, severe shortness of
  breath, dizziness or fainting, an irregular heartbeat, or sharp pain.
- The Low-Impact Basics plan is general fitness, not physiotherapy or rehabilitation.
- Rep counts, form feedback and calorie numbers are estimates and can be wrong. Camera form feedback
  cannot see everything a coach can; use the instructions and your own judgement.
- Exercise in a clear, safe space with stable furniture when exercises use a chair, step or wall.

The same text is shown in onboarding (and must be accepted) and under Settings → About.
