### Description

FormFit turns your phone into a personal trainer that watches your form.

Prop your phone up, press start, and FormFit's on-device AI counts your reps, spots common form
mistakes ("chest up", "knees out", "hips down") and coaches you out loud while your music keeps
playing. Video never leaves your phone.

• 11 home workout plans — 6-Pack Abs Builder, Beginner Foundations, Full-Body Fat Burn, Bodyweight
Muscle Builder, Mobility, a 15-minute daily express, low-impact basics and more — each with a
day-by-day schedule, weekly progression and lighter recovery weeks.
• 54 exercises with step-by-step guides, common mistakes, easier/harder options and original
animated demonstrations.
• Hands-free workouts: countdowns, automatic sets, rest timers and voice cues.
• Progress you can see: streaks, weekly charts, a training calendar and personal bests.
• Works offline. Create a free account to back up and sync, or train as a guest.
• Privacy first: no ads, no tracking, export or delete your data any time.

FormFit offers general fitness guidance and is not medical advice. Consult a doctor before starting
a new exercise programme.
