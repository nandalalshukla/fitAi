# FormFit Privacy Policy

_Draft for review by a qualified lawyer before publication. Last updated: 24 October 2026._

FormFit ("we") makes the FormFit mobile app. This policy explains what data the app handles, why,
and your choices. Replace bracketed items before publishing.

**Contact:** [company legal name], [postal address], [privacy email].

## 1. Camera

The camera is used only while you train, to estimate body keypoints (joint positions) so the app
can count reps and check form. **Video and images are processed in real time on your device and are
never recorded, stored or uploaded.** Only rep counts and form scores are kept.

## 2. Data we store

| Data                                                                                           | Where                                           | Why                                      | Legal basis (GDPR)                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Profile answers (goal, fitness level, equipment, days per week, units)                         | Your device; our servers if you have an account | Recommend and run your plan              | Contract                              |
| Workout history (exercises, sets, reps, hold times, form scores, durations, calorie estimates) | Your device; our servers if you have an account | Show progress, sync between your devices | Contract                              |
| Settings (voice, reminders, appearance)                                                        | Your device; our servers if you have an account | Keep your preferences                    | Contract                              |
| Email address (email, Google or Apple sign-in)                                                 | Our authentication provider                     | Sign-in and account recovery             | Contract                              |
| Anonymous usage events (e.g. "workout finished", screen views) with a random install id        | Our analytics provider                          | Improve the app                          | Legitimate interest — you can opt out |
| Crash reports (stack traces, device model, OS, app version — scrubbed of emails and tokens)    | Our crash-reporting provider                    | Fix bugs                                 | Legitimate interest — you can opt out |

Workout data can relate to your health. We use it only to provide the app to you; we do not sell
it, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers. There is no third-party tracking or ads.

As a guest, your data stays on your device (or under an anonymous account) until you create an account.

## 3. Processors

- **Supabase** (database and authentication) — region: [EU/US region].
- **Sentry** (crash reports) — only if enabled in the build; you can opt out in Settings → Privacy.
- **PostHog** (analytics, if enabled) — anonymous install id only; you can opt out in Settings → Privacy.
- **Apple / Google** — if you choose Sign in with Apple or Google.

## 4. Retention

Your account data is kept while your account exists. Deleting your account (Settings → Account →
Delete account and data) permanently removes your profile and workout history from our servers
immediately and from backups within [30] days. Anonymous analytics are kept for up to [12] months.

## 5. Your rights

You can access and export your data (Settings → Account → Export my data), correct it, delete it
(Delete account), and object to analytics/crash reporting (Settings → Privacy). Depending on where
you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA) you also have rights to restrict processing, portability and
to complain to your data protection authority. Contact [privacy email].

## 6. Security

Data in transit uses HTTPS. Sign-in tokens are encrypted on your device with a key held in the
iOS Keychain / Android Keystore. Server data is protected by row-level security so each user can
only access their own records.

## 7. Children

FormFit is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## 8. Changes

We will update this page and the in-app summary when practices change, and notify you in the app
for material changes.
